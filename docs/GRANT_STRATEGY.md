# Grant Strategy — NeuroAdapt AI

## 1. Positioning

NeuroAdapt AI should be presented as an impact-driven RT3D rehabilitation platform that is being developed in Unity and is designed around measurable, patient-centered rehabilitation workflows.

The grant narrative should avoid claiming clinical efficacy before evidence exists. The strongest credible positioning is:

> **We are building an accessible immersive rehabilitation platform that adapts therapeutic tasks to user performance while generating structured evidence for clinicians and researchers.**

## 2. Unity for Humanity alignment

Unity's public FAQ states that eligible projects must be impact-driven, use RT3D, and be in production beyond conceptualization with a prototype or demo. The published rubric evaluates Vision, Impact, Inclusion, and Viability at 25% each. The FAQ also states that applications require a project video sample or trailer, short essays, and optionally a pitch deck.

Source: https://unity.com/humanity

### Vision

Lead with the lived problem: rehabilitation can be repetitive, inaccessible, difficult to personalize, and dependent on scarce specialist time. The unique perspective is to make the therapeutic environment adaptive and accessible without turning AI into an autonomous clinical decision-maker.

### Impact

Define a small number of primary outcomes and a transparent measurement plan. Separate:

- product metrics: sessions, completion, retention, usability;
- rehabilitation metrics: task performance and clinician-selected measures;
- patient-reported outcomes: pain, confidence, perceived effort, quality of life where appropriate;
- equity metrics: accessibility and deployment feasibility in constrained environments.

### Inclusion

Demonstrate co-design with people who have lived experience. Do not merely state that an advisory group exists. Document roles, testing cycles, accessibility requirements, and how feedback changed the product.

### Viability

Show a staged production plan, scope controls, technical architecture, research governance, and a budget tied to deliverables. The prototype is the critical credibility milestone.

## 3. Claims discipline

The following wording should be avoided unless supported by evidence:

- "clinically proven"
- "certified medical device"
- "guarantees pain reduction"
- "eliminates seizures"
- "predicts recovery accurately"
- "HIPAA compliant" or "GDPR compliant" as a blanket software claim
- "ready for global clinical deployment"

Preferred wording:

- "designed to evaluate"
- "proposed intervention"
- "research hypothesis"
- "prototype"
- "planned validation"
- "subject to clinical and regulatory review"

## 4. 2026 submission timing note

The 2026 Unity for Humanity application window has closed. Unity announced a February 20, 2026 deadline and currently states that applications will open again in 2027. This repository should therefore be maintained as a reusable submission package rather than described as an open 2026 application.

## 5. Minimum evidence package before the next application

1. Runnable Unity prototype.
2. Short demonstration video with subtitles.
3. Architecture diagram matching the actual implementation.
4. Accessibility demonstration.
5. A.U.R.A. baseline adaptation demo.
6. Research/measurement protocol.
7. Preliminary usability evidence.
8. Named legal applicant and documented governance.
9. Realistic budget tied to milestones.
10. Clear distinction between prototype evidence and future clinical validation.
